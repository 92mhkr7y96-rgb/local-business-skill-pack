# Local Business Marketing Plan Examples

## Example 1: Basic Example

Input: Coffee shop wants a 30-day plan for weekday lunch traffic using Instagram, Google Business, and in-store signage.

Output focus: weekly themes, channel strategy, local content ideas, simple offers, and risk notes.

## Example 2: Realistic Business Example

Input: Fitness studio wants a new member campaign before summer, with limited budget and only 3 posting days per week.

Output focus: realistic content rhythm, trial-class promotion, referral idea, local partnership, and no growth guarantees.
