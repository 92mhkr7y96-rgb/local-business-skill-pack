# Customer Message Reply Examples

## Example 1: Basic Example

Input: Customer asks whether a coffee shop has oat milk and outdoor seating.

Output focus: polite reply, known facts, follow-up question, and no invented details.

## Example 2: Realistic Business Example

Input: Customer complains about a delayed pet grooming appointment and asks what can be done.

Output focus: empathetic response, private follow-up, escalation note, and no unapproved compensation.
