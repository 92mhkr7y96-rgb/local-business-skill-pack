# Business Profile Optimizer

Optimizes local business profile copy, descriptions, service highlights, FAQs, CTAs, and completeness notes.

## Suitable Scenarios

- Google Business profile rewrite
- Yelp business description
- Xiaohongshu business profile
- Instagram bio and highlights
- Local service listing optimization

## Steps

1. Provide business facts, services, audience, tone, hours, contact method, and existing text.
2. Generate optimized profile copy.
3. Verify facts and platform rules.
4. Publish after owner review.

## Input Fields

Business name, type, location, services, audience, tone, hours, contact method, existing text, avoid list, and language.

## Output Fields

Positioning summary, description, short bio, service highlights, FAQs, CTAs, checklist, keywords, risky claims, and final profile draft.

## Common Mistakes

- Inventing ratings or awards
- Claiming unverified rankings
- Adding private customer details

## Safety Reminder

Do not fabricate credentials, ratings, reviews, awards, rankings, or customer data.
