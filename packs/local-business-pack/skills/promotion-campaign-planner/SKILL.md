# Skill: promotion-campaign-planner

## 中文注释

这个 skill 用于促销活动策划，帮助本地商家设计节日、新品、会员、淡季和社区活动。输出必须明确活动条款、时间、渠道和限制，不能制造虚假折扣、虚假稀缺或保证销售增长。

## Purpose

Create clear and realistic local promotion campaigns.

## Best for

Holiday promotions, new service launches, member campaigns, referral programs, and slow-season offers.

## Not for

Fake discounts, deceptive urgency, hidden terms, guaranteed sales, or misleading customer claims.

## Input

- Business type
- Campaign goal
- Target customers
- Offer
- Campaign dates
- Channels
- Budget
- Staff capacity
- Restrictions
- Language

## Output

1. Campaign summary
2. Target customers
3. Offer structure
4. Campaign timeline
5. Channel plan
6. Content ideas
7. CTA options
8. Staff and operations notes
9. Measurement plan
10. Terms and restrictions
11. Risky wording to avoid

## Process

1. Clarify goal and audience.
2. Make the offer understandable.
3. Build timeline and channel plan.
4. Add content and CTA options.
5. Add terms, restrictions, and risk notes.

## Quality Bar

- Offer is clear.
- Terms are visible.
- Timeline is realistic.
- Channel plan fits the business.
- No fake urgency or fake discounts.
- No guaranteed growth claims.

## Example Input

Business type: fitness studio
Campaign goal: new member trial class
Offer: owner-provided trial discount
Campaign dates: first two weeks of June
Channels: Instagram, flyers, Google Business

## Example Output

Campaign angle: summer starter trial class with clear booking steps, limited campaign dates, and staff-capacity notes.

## Safety / Compliance Notes

Do not fabricate discounts, availability, customer results, claims, reviews, sales growth, private customer data, or urgency. Verify offer terms before publishing.
