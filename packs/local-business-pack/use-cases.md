# Local Business Skill Pack Use Cases

## Typical Use Cases

1. Create a monthly marketing plan for a local business.
2. Reply to positive, neutral, and negative customer reviews.
3. Design service menus and customer-facing service descriptions.
4. Plan promotions for holidays, new services, memberships, or slow seasons.
5. Reply to customer DMs about inquiries, booking, service details, and after-sales issues.
6. Optimize business profiles on Google Business, Yelp, Xiaohongshu, Instagram, and local listing platforms.

## When to Use Each Skill

- `local-business-marketing-plan`: use when planning monthly or seasonal local marketing.
- `review-reply-generator`: use when replying to public reviews without exposing private customer data.
- `service-menu-generator`: use when organizing service items, add-ons, and packages.
- `promotion-campaign-planner`: use when designing discounts, bundles, or event campaigns.
- `customer-message-reply`: use when handling customer inquiries, booking questions, and support messages.
- `business-profile-optimizer`: use when rewriting business descriptions, service highlights, FAQs, and profile content.

## Recommended Combined Workflow

```text
Business positioning
-> Service menu design
-> Marketing plan
-> Promotion campaign
-> Customer message reply
-> Review reply
-> Business profile optimization
```

## 中文使用说明

这套 Pack 适合本地小店和服务业商家，用于把日常运营内容整理成可执行的营销方案、服务菜单、促销活动、客户回复和主页优化文案。请使用真实商家信息，不要编造评论、资质、销量、客户增长或私人客户信息。

## English Usage Notes

Use this pack to draft and organize local business operations content. Keep all business facts, service details, promotions, reviews, and customer replies truthful and privacy-safe.

## Boundaries

Do not use this pack for fake reviews, guaranteed sales, misleading discounts, private customer data, fake credentials, or deceptive marketing.
