# Safety Guidelines

This pack helps draft local business marketing and operations content, but every output must be checked before use.

## Rules

- Do not fabricate customer reviews.
- Do not guarantee sales or customer growth.
- Do not create deceptive marketing claims.
- Do not add private customer data.
- Do not add API keys.
- Do not add private data.
- Do not fabricate facts, credentials, claims, metrics, or user data.
- Do not directly push to main.
- Create a pull request for human review.

## Additional Guidance

- Verify promotion terms, dates, pricing, and availability.
- Do not disclose customer names, phone numbers, addresses, order details, or health/service history.
- For regulated services, verify local laws and platform policies.
- Mark assumptions clearly when business details are missing.

## Disclaimer

This skill pack helps draft, organize, and improve local business marketing and operations content. It does not guarantee sales, customer growth, bookings, ranking improvement, platform approval, or business results. Users must verify facts, offers, privacy requirements, local regulations, and platform policies before publishing.
