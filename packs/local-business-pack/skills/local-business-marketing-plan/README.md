# Local Business Marketing Plan

Creates realistic monthly marketing plans for local businesses.

## Suitable Scenarios

- Monthly local marketing planning
- Seasonal promotions
- Local awareness campaigns
- Repeat customer campaigns
- Community partnership ideas

## Steps

1. Provide business type, location, audience, services, goal, budget, and channels.
2. Generate a monthly plan.
3. Review claims, offers, dates, and local rules.
4. Adapt the plan to staff capacity.

## Input Fields

Business type, location, target customers, services/products, monthly goal, budget, channels, dates, offers, avoid list, and language.

## Output Fields

Plan summary, customer segments, goals, channel strategy, weekly actions, content ideas, promotions, partnerships, budget notes, risks, and checklist.

## Common Mistakes

- Treating goals as guaranteed results
- Planning beyond staff capacity
- Making unsupported claims

## Safety Reminder

Do not fabricate metrics, customer stories, sales claims, or private customer data.
