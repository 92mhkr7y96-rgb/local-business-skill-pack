# Skill: service-menu-generator

## 中文注释

这个 skill 用于服务菜单设计，帮助商家整理服务项目、价格区间、套餐、加购项和顾客可读描述。不能编造资质、服务效果、优惠、库存、可预约时间或不真实的价格。

## Purpose

Design service menus that are clear, customer-friendly, and easy to publish.

## Best for

Nail salons, fitness studios, pet grooming stores, coffee shops, photography studios, and local service providers.

## Not for

Fake credentials, misleading results, fake prices, hidden terms, or unsupported service claims.

## Input

- Business type
- Service list
- Price range
- Duration
- Add-ons
- Target customers
- Brand tone
- Constraints
- Language

## Output

1. Service menu overview
2. Service categories
3. Service item descriptions
4. Price range notes
5. Add-on suggestions
6. Package options
7. Customer-facing menu copy
8. FAQ opportunities
9. Upsell and cross-sell ideas
10. Risky claims to avoid
11. Review checklist

## Process

1. Group services into clear categories.
2. Rewrite service descriptions for customers.
3. Add price and duration notes where provided.
4. Suggest add-ons and packages.
5. Flag unsupported claims and unclear terms.

## Quality Bar

- Menu is easy to understand.
- Service descriptions are specific.
- Price and duration are not invented.
- Add-ons are relevant.
- Risky claims are flagged.
- Customer can compare options quickly.

## Example Input

Business type: nail salon
Services: classic manicure, gel manicure, nail art
Price range: provided by owner
Target customers: nearby office workers and weekend customers

## Example Output

Service category: everyday manicure options. Menu copy should clearly separate classic polish, gel polish, and nail art add-ons.

## Safety / Compliance Notes

Do not fabricate prices, credentials, certifications, service outcomes, availability, customer results, or private customer data.
