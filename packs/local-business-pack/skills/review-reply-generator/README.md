# Review Reply Generator

Generates privacy-safe public replies and optional private follow-up notes for customer reviews.

## Suitable Scenarios

- Positive review replies
- Negative review replies
- Neutral review replies
- Google/Yelp/Xiaohongshu reviews
- Escalation-friendly responses

## Steps

1. Provide review text, platform, sentiment, known facts, and tone.
2. Generate a public reply.
3. Remove private details.
4. Publish only after business review.

## Input Fields

Business type, platform, review text, sentiment, known facts, tone, policy, avoid list, and language.

## Output Fields

Review summary, reply goal, public reply, private follow-up, tone notes, avoid list, escalation suggestion, risky wording, and checklist.

## Common Mistakes

- Mentioning private customer details
- Arguing publicly
- Inventing what happened

## Safety Reminder

Do not fabricate reviews, customer details, service history, compensation, or legal facts.
