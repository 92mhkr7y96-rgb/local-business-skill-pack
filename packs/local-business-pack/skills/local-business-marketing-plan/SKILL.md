# Skill: local-business-marketing-plan

## 中文注释

这个 skill 用于本地店铺月度营销方案，适合咖啡店、美甲店、健身房、宠物店等小微商家。输出应包含目标客户、渠道、活动、内容节奏和执行清单，但不能承诺销售增长、获客增长、排名提升或平台审核通过。

## Purpose

Create realistic local marketing plans for small shops, service businesses, and studios.

## Best for

Monthly planning, seasonal campaigns, local awareness, repeat customer programs, and community marketing.

## Not for

Guaranteed growth, deceptive claims, fake customer stories, fake metrics, or private customer data use.

## Input

- Business type
- Location
- Target customers
- Services or products
- Monthly goal
- Budget
- Channels
- Important dates
- Existing offers
- Things to avoid
- Language

## Output

1. Marketing plan summary
2. Target customer segments
3. Monthly goals
4. Channel strategy
5. Weekly action plan
6. Content ideas
7. Promotion ideas
8. Local partnership ideas
9. Budget notes
10. Risks and claims to avoid
11. Review checklist

## Process

1. Clarify the business, customers, and realistic goal.
2. Match channels to local customer behavior.
3. Build a weekly plan with content and campaign actions.
4. Add promotion and local partnership ideas.
5. Flag unsupported claims, privacy risks, and unrealistic promises.

## Quality Bar

- Uses realistic local tactics.
- Provides executable weekly actions.
- Separates marketing goals from guaranteed results.
- Avoids fake customer feedback or fake metrics.
- Includes budget and channel notes.
- Includes risks to avoid.

## Example Input

Business type: local coffee shop
Location: neighborhood near office buildings
Monthly goal: increase weekday lunch-hour visits
Channels: Instagram, Google Business, in-store posters

## Example Output

Weekly theme: office lunch break coffee and light snacks. Use short menu posts, Google Business updates, and in-store signage with clear offer terms.

## Safety / Compliance Notes

Do not guarantee sales, bookings, customer growth, reviews, rankings, or platform approval. Do not fabricate facts, claims, metrics, credentials, customer feedback, or private data.
