# Local Business Skill Pack

## English

Local Business Skill Pack is an open-source AI workflow skill pack for small local businesses, service shops, and small studios. It helps merchants plan marketing, package services, design promotions, reply to customer messages and reviews, and optimize business profiles.

It is designed for coffee shops, nail salons, barbershops, restaurants, gyms, pet shops, photography studios, and local service providers.

This pack is not for fake reviews, deceptive marketing, private customer data misuse, fabricated credentials, or guaranteed sales and customer growth claims.

## 中文

Local Business Skill Pack 是一个面向本地小店、服务业商家和小型工作室的开源 AI 工作流 Skill Pack，用于帮助商家完成营销方案、服务菜单、促销活动、客户私信回复、评论回复和商家主页优化。

它适合咖啡店、美甲店、理发店、餐厅、健身房、宠物店、摄影工作室和本地服务商家。

它不适合伪造评论、欺骗式营销、滥用客户隐私、编造资质，或承诺获客、销售增长和平台排名结果。
