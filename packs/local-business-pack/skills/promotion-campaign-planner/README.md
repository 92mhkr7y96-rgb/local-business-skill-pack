# Promotion Campaign Planner

Plans local business promotions with clear offers, timelines, channels, CTAs, and terms.

## Suitable Scenarios

- Holiday promotions
- New service launches
- Membership offers
- Referral campaigns
- Slow-season campaigns

## Steps

1. Provide business type, goal, customers, offer, dates, channels, budget, capacity, and restrictions.
2. Generate campaign plan.
3. Verify terms and availability.
4. Publish after owner review.

## Input Fields

Business type, goal, target customers, offer, dates, channels, budget, capacity, restrictions, and language.

## Output Fields

Summary, audience, offer, timeline, channel plan, content ideas, CTA, operations notes, measurement plan, terms, and risky wording.

## Common Mistakes

- Hiding terms
- Inventing discounts
- Overpromising results

## Safety Reminder

Do not create deceptive offers, fake urgency, fake scarcity, or guaranteed results.
