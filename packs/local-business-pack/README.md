# Local Business Skill Pack

Local Business Skill Pack is an open-source AI workflow pack for local shops, service businesses, and small studios.

It helps merchants organize positioning, service menus, marketing plans, promotions, customer message replies, review replies, and business profile optimization.

## Best For

- Coffee shops
- Nail salons
- Barbershops
- Restaurants
- Gyms and fitness studios
- Pet shops and grooming stores
- Photography studios
- Local service businesses

## Not For

- Fabricating customer reviews
- Guaranteeing sales or customer growth
- Deceptive marketing claims
- Adding private customer data
- Fake credentials, fake awards, fake metrics, or fake user feedback

## Core Skills

| Skill | 中文说明 | Main Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [local-business-marketing-plan](skills/local-business-marketing-plan/) | 本地店铺营销方案 | Build monthly marketing plans for local shops. |
| [review-reply-generator](skills/review-reply-generator/) | 评论回复生成 | Reply to Google, Yelp, Xiaohongshu, and other public reviews. |
| [service-menu-generator](skills/service-menu-generator/) | 服务菜单设计 | Package service items, price ranges, add-ons, and customer-facing descriptions. |
| [promotion-campaign-planner](skills/promotion-campaign-planner/) | 促销活动策划 | Plan seasonal, launch, member, and local community campaigns. |
| [customer-message-reply](skills/customer-message-reply/) | 客户私信回复 | Reply to inquiries, booking questions, after-sales issues, and service questions. |
| [business-profile-optimizer](skills/business-profile-optimizer/) | 商家主页优化 | Improve Google Business, Yelp, Xiaohongshu, Instagram, or local listing profiles. |

## Recommended Workflow

```text
Business positioning
-> Service menu design
-> Marketing plan
-> Promotion campaign
-> Customer message reply
-> Review reply
-> Business profile optimization
```
