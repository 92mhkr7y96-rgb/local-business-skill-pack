# Review Reply Generator Examples

## Example 1: Basic Example

Input: A happy customer says the coffee was great and staff were friendly.

Output focus: warm short reply, gratitude, invitation to return, no fake claims.

## Example 2: Realistic Business Example

Input: A nail salon receives a negative review about appointment wait time and unclear communication.

Output focus: calm public reply, private follow-up invitation, privacy-safe language, no fabricated details.
