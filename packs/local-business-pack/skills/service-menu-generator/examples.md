# Service Menu Generator Examples

## Example 1: Basic Example

Input: Coffee shop wants to organize drinks, pastries, lunch sets, and seasonal add-ons.

Output focus: service/product categories, customer-facing descriptions, add-ons, and risk notes.

## Example 2: Realistic Business Example

Input: Pet grooming store has bath, trim, nail care, and add-on services with owner-provided price ranges.

Output focus: service menu structure, package options, FAQ ideas, and no invented availability or results.
