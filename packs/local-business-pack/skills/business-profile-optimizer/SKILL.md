# Skill: business-profile-optimizer

## 中文注释

这个 skill 用于商家主页优化，适合 Google Business、Yelp、小红书、Instagram、本地平台等主页。输出应优化商家介绍、服务亮点、FAQ 和 CTA，但不能编造资质、奖项、评论、评分、销量、排名或服务结果。

## Purpose

Improve local business profile copy, service highlights, FAQs, and calls to action.

## Best for

Google Business, Yelp, Xiaohongshu, Instagram, local listing pages, and service directory profiles.

## Not for

Fake credentials, fake ratings, fake reviews, fake awards, misleading service claims, or private data.

## Input

- Business name
- Business type
- Location
- Services
- Target customers
- Brand tone
- Opening hours
- Contact or booking method
- Existing profile text
- Things to avoid
- Language

## Output

1. Profile positioning summary
2. Business description
3. Short bio
4. Service highlights
5. FAQ suggestions
6. CTA options
7. Profile completeness checklist
8. Keyword suggestions
9. Risky claims to avoid
10. Final profile draft

## Process

1. Extract real business facts.
2. Rewrite description and short bio.
3. Highlight services and customer fit.
4. Add FAQ, CTA, and keyword suggestions.
5. Flag unsupported claims and missing data.

## Quality Bar

- Profile is clear and customer-friendly.
- Uses only provided business facts.
- Service highlights are specific.
- Does not invent reviews, ratings, awards, credentials, or results.
- Includes completeness checklist.

## Example Input

Business name: neighborhood coffee shop
Business type: coffee shop
Services: espresso, seasonal drinks, pastries, light lunch
Target customers: nearby residents and office workers
Platform: Google Business

## Example Output

Short bio: A neighborhood coffee shop serving espresso drinks, seasonal beverages, pastries, and light lunch options for nearby residents and office workers.

## Safety / Compliance Notes

Do not fabricate ratings, awards, credentials, claims, customer reviews, rankings, sales, or private customer data.
