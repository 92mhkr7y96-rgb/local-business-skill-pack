# Service Menu Generator

Packages service items, descriptions, add-ons, and bundles for local businesses.

## Suitable Scenarios

- New service menu
- Price list rewrite
- Package design
- Add-on planning
- Profile service section optimization

## Steps

1. Provide service list, prices, durations, add-ons, audience, tone, and constraints.
2. Generate menu structure.
3. Verify price and service details.
4. Publish after owner review.

## Input Fields

Business type, service list, price range, duration, add-ons, customers, tone, constraints, and language.

## Output Fields

Overview, categories, item descriptions, price notes, add-ons, packages, menu copy, FAQs, upsell ideas, risky claims, and checklist.

## Common Mistakes

- Inventing prices or service duration
- Overpromising service results
- Hiding terms or restrictions

## Safety Reminder

Do not fabricate credentials, claims, prices, availability, or customer outcomes.
