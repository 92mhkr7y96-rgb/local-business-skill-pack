# Promotion Campaign Planner Examples

## Example 1: Basic Example

Input: Coffee shop wants a holiday drink promotion for one week.

Output focus: offer structure, social posts, in-store signage, CTA, and terms.

## Example 2: Realistic Business Example

Input: Nail salon wants a new seasonal design campaign with limited staff capacity and appointment booking requirements.

Output focus: clear terms, booking CTA, channel plan, capacity notes, and no guaranteed sales claims.
