# Customer Message Reply

Drafts polite customer message replies for inquiries, bookings, complaints, and after-sales issues.

## Suitable Scenarios

- Booking inquiries
- Pricing questions
- Service details
- Complaint replies
- Follow-up messages

## Steps

1. Provide customer message, known facts, policies, tone, and avoid list.
2. Generate reply.
3. Verify availability, pricing, and policy.
4. Send after owner review.

## Input Fields

Business type, customer message, intent, known facts, service details, booking policy, tone, escalation rules, avoid list, and language.

## Output Fields

Intent summary, reply, follow-up questions, booking/service details, escalation notes, tone notes, privacy warnings, risky wording, and checklist.

## Common Mistakes

- Promising unavailable times
- Sharing private customer information
- Giving unapproved compensation

## Safety Reminder

Do not invent availability, prices, policies, compensation, or private customer details.
